## Step 6: Hub, Digi-ID, DigiStamp

- **Hub per wallet: PARTIAL.** Upgrade 4.1.3 test signed in (HTTP 200) and the Chat tab showed "Signed in with wallet Upgrade 4.1.3 test". Switching to Wallet 1 showed signed out; switching back showed the Upgrade account again with no sign-in prompt. A second account could not be signed in: the server refused Wallet 1 (HTTP 403, same as on 4.1.4) and Seedauth emu28 testnet (HTTP 403, 88-byte body, the server's per-network new-account limit). Not a wallet fault.
- **Digi-ID identity per wallet: PASS for restored wallets.** No test site was available, so the Digi-ID approval screen (opened with a `digiid://` link, nothing approved) was read instead: for a restored wallet it lists the identity addresses the wallet would sign with. Hashed and compared: the five restored wallets show five pairwise-different pairs, and Upgrade 4.1.3 test showed the same pair before and after several switches. Wallet 1 (legacy) and a created wallet show no identity picker, so their addresses are not on screen; the Hub answers above show Wallet 1 differs from Upgrade 4.1.3 test and from Seedauth emu28 testnet.
- **DigiStamp: PARTIAL.** The in-app Market WebView data is erased on every switch: `WalletSwitcher: web session end confirmed=true` on each switch, `app_webview/` absent after the switch and fresh cookies created on the next visit; `dgb_web_session_owner` names the wallet that opened it. But **Market → DigiStamp "Sign in" opens an external Custom Tab (Samsung Internet on this phone)**, not the in-app WebView, and the Digi-ID sign-in and the resulting DigiStamp session live there. The app cannot clear that browser's cookies, so a DigiStamp session started this way is not ended by a switch. Not completed on device (no DigiStamp sign-in was approved). See Concerns.

## Step 8: PIN-failure wipe (API 33)

Fresh `dgb-test-api33` (no screen lock), three wallets (first-run create + two Add → Create),
Security → "Wipe After 10 Failed PINs" turned on.

- Security text: the confirmation says 10 wrong PINs "PERMANENTLY erase every wallet on this
  device", and the row then reads "Enabled: every wallet is erased after 10 wrong PINs". PASS.
- Ten wrong PINs, through the cooldowns (none for 1–3, then 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 60 min ×3;
  about 3 h 36 min on the monotonic clock): at the 10th the app logged
  `FreshStartAfterWipe: ending this process, starting a fresh one` and the new process started
  at `onboarding?mode=first_run walletState=NoWallet`. PASS.
- `run-as … ls shared_prefs`: no per-wallet seed record (`dgb_wallet_seed_<id>`) remains, and
  `dgb_wallet_seed.xml` is present but an empty map (65 bytes, written at the moment of the wipe).
  `dgb_wallets.xml` is an empty map too, and two `_<id>` files of one of the erased wallets
  (`dgb_digiscope_<id>`, `dgb_hub_session_<id>`) remain as empty maps. No seed data survives, but
  the brief's literal check ("no `dgb_wallet_seed*`") **fails**: the empty file is still listed.
  See Concerns.

## Concerns found

1. **DigiStamp sign-in leaves the app.** See Step 6. The switch erases the in-app WebView, but the Market's DigiStamp sign-in runs in an external Custom Tab, so that session carries across a switch. Severity: Low/Medium (identity linkability between wallets on DigiStamp).
2. **Add-mode copy:** after verifying a new wallet's words in add mode, the screen still says "Now let's set up a PIN." with a "Set Up PIN" button (`verify_success_body`, `verify_setup_pin` in `SeedVerifyScreen.kt`), although no PIN is set (the next step is the passphrase choice, then the add commits). Low.
3. **Upgrade listing deviation:** two prefs files beyond `dgb_wallets.xml` appear after the upgrade (`dgb_web_session_owner.xml`, and `dgb_digiscope.xml` as an empty map). Low; recorded against the brief's literal check.
4. **Wipe leaves empty files behind:** after the PIN-failure wipe, `dgb_wallet_seed.xml`,
   `dgb_wallets.xml` and two `_<id>` prefs of an erased wallet are still listed as empty maps.
   Nothing secret is left, but the names show that wallets existed, and the brief's check
   expects no `dgb_wallet_seed*` at all. Also logged at the wipe:
   `IdentitySessionEraser: web session: cookie store reported removal=false` (the WebView had
   never been opened on that emulator, so there may have been nothing to remove). Low.
5. **Renamed wallet in the notification:** the running sync notification kept the old name ("Wallet 5") after a rename until the next process start. Low.
